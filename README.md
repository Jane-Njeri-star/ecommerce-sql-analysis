# E-Commerce Revenue & Customer Growth Analysis

## Business Question

**How is the business performing, and what is driving revenue growth?**

This project analyzes e-commerce sales performance with a focus on two key areas:

* **Revenue performance and growth over time**
* **Customer growth and the addition of new customers**

The goal was to move beyond reporting sales figures and understand whether the business is building sustainable growth through increasing revenue and expanding its customer base.

---

## Business Context

Revenue performance fluctuated throughout the period analyzed, creating a need to understand where growth was occurring and where performance was weakening.

One of the key findings was that **April recorded the strongest average revenue at 1,077**, providing a useful benchmark for evaluating performance across the rest of the year.

The analysis also identified approximately **99,000 unique customers**, highlighting significant potential for continued growth through customer acquisition and retention.

Rather than treating revenue as an isolated metric, this project looks at the relationship between **revenue performance, customer growth, and business improvement**.

---

## Key Questions

The analysis focused on answering:

1. **How is revenue performing over time?**
2. **Which periods demonstrate the strongest and weakest revenue performance?**
3. **Is the business showing signs of revenue improvement?**
4. **How many unique customers does the business serve?**
5. **Is the customer base expanding?**
6. **What opportunities exist to improve revenue and retain newly acquired customers?**
7. **What performance areas require further investigation?**

---

## Key Insights

### 📈 Revenue Performance

Revenue fluctuated throughout the year, with **April achieving the highest average revenue of 1,077**.

Although performance declined afterward, the subsequent upward movement indicates a period of recovery and improvement. April therefore provides a useful benchmark for assessing whether future performance is returning to—and eventually exceeding—the previous high.

### 👥 Customer Growth

The business recorded approximately **99,000 unique customers**, representing a substantial customer base and an important opportunity for future revenue growth.

Growing the customer base is valuable, but the next analytical question is whether these customers are returning and contributing to sustained revenue rather than generating only one-time purchases.

### 🎯 Growth Opportunity

The analysis suggests two complementary priorities:

**Acquire customers → Retain customers → Increase revenue per customer**

Future analysis should investigate customer retention, repeat purchasing behavior, and the factors behind periods of weaker revenue performance.

---

## Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the business should:

* Establish a revenue target **above the April benchmark of 1,077**.
* Investigate the factors that contributed to April's strong performance.
* Identify what drove weaker-performing periods and determine whether the issue relates to **customer retention, demand, supply, product performance, or other operational factors**.
* Focus on converting new customers into repeat customers.
* Monitor revenue and customer growth together rather than evaluating either metric in isolation.
* Continue tracking performance against the established benchmark to measure whether improvements are sustained.

## Analytical Approach

The project followed a simple business intelligence workflow:

**Data → Cleaning → SQL Analysis → Excel Analysis → Power BI → Insights → Recommendations**

### 1. Data Preparation

The data was cleaned and prepared for analysis to ensure consistency and usability across the different analytical tools.

### 2. SQL Analysis

SQL was used to explore the underlying data and answer business questions around:

* Revenue performance
* Customer activity
* Customer counts
* Sales trends
* Business performance patterns

SQL provided the foundation for identifying patterns that could then be explored visually.

### 3. Excel Analysis

Excel was used for additional analysis, calculations, and exploration of the dataset.

This helped validate findings and develop a clearer understanding of revenue and customer trends.

### 4. Power BI

Power BI was used to transform the analysis into an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

The dashboard focuses on making revenue and customer performance easier to monitor and interpret for decision-making.
